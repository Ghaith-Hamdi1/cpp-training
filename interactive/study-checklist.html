Move semantics deep dive (after the interview)

Not needed for Thursday. When you want to master move semantics, follow the two reading guides: Effective Modern C++ move semantics path (effective.pdf) and the C++ Move Semantics guide, Tier 1 (semant.pdf). The Josuttis book covers what Meyers leaves out: the moved-from state, value categories and writing move operations. About 75 pages of Josuttis plus a few Meyers items.
