Final review

Move semantics deep dive (optional)

Not needed for a first pass. When you want to master move semantics, follow the two reading guides: Effective Modern C++ move semantics path (books/Effective-Modern-Cpp-Meyers.pdf) and the C++ Move Semantics guide, Tier 1 (books/Cpp-Move-Semantics-The-Complete-Guide-Josuttis.pdf). The Josuttis book covers what Meyers leaves out: the moved-from state, value categories and writing move operations. About 75 pages of Josuttis plus a few Meyers items.

Books, only to fill gaps

Do not read any of these books front to back. The reading guides in the repo point to the few sections you need: threading (books/Cpp-Concurrency-in-Action-2nd-Edition-Williams.pdf), foundations: pointers, OOP, templates, STL (books/Programming-Principles-and-Practice-Using-Cpp-3rd-Edition-Stroustrup.pdf), modern C++ items (books/Effective-Modern-Cpp-Meyers.pdf) and move semantics in depth (books/Cpp-Move-Semantics-The-Complete-Guide-Josuttis.pdf).
