Suggested order

Each lesson builds on the ones before it, so going in order works best. Do a lesson, then its quiz.

Where this comes from

  • Your own notes in this repo: the training notes index (docs/ and examples/) and the learncpp.com recaps.
  • Runnable versions of the lesson snippets (complete programs you can compile): examples/lessons on GitHub.
  • Feature reference: AnthonyCalandra/modern-cpp-features (a cheat sheet of C++11 to C++23 features).
  • Book-style depth: changkun/modern-cpp-tutorial (modern C++ explained chapter by chapter, including its concurrency chapter).

The lessons are original explanations written for this repo; the two projects above were used as references for which features and concepts matter.
