C++ Interview Prep

Seven interactive lessons, each with its own quiz, taking you from pointers to thread pools. Everything runs in your browser from these files: no install, no internet needed, and your progress is saved on this device.

How each lesson works
  • Step-through visualisers show memory changing one line at a time (pointers, copies, moves, shared_ptr counts, vector reallocation).
  • Simulators let you play the scheduler to cause (and fix) data races and deadlocks yourself.
  • Predict-the-output exercises and an interview cheat sheet at the end of every lesson: say each answer out loud before you open it.
  • Tick "I understand this section" as you go. Quizzes explain every answer and let you retry only the questions you missed.
