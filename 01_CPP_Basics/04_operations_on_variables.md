# Operations on variables

*(breadcrumb text on this slide reads "Operations on varibles" — typo preserved from source)*

- Types of operations:
  - Unary: unique operand (e.g. `-x`)
  - Binary: two operands (e.g. `x + y`)
  - Ternary (only one conditional operator: `condition ? expr1 : expr2;`)
  - Nullary (only one: `throw`)
- Chaining operators, e.g. `x + y * z`
  - Follow operator priority / use `(` and `)`
- Result of operations vary with operator
